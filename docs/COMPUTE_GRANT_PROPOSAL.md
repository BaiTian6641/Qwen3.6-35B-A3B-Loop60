## 1. Research Pitch & Motivation

Scaling test-time reasoning via long chains of thought alone hits severe KV-cache and serial-token bottlenecks, while training looped or ultra-long-context transformers from scratch requires industrial-scale pretraining budgets. We investigate a sharp, under-explored alternative: **can a frozen `35B` (`A3B`) NVFP4 hybrid MoE backbone (`unsloth/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`, `32.212B` routed experts 100% frozen and SHA-256 verified) be retrofitted with `< 0.6%` trainable parameters into a runtime-scalable `88`-virtual-layer looped reasoner supporting `1,048,576`-token (`1M`) context inside `10.65 GiB` of context state?** Our clean v2 architecture combines three orthogonal mechanisms: (1) a calibrated `[12,36)` ETD sandwich loop with mHC `LoopBridge` and per-pass router adapters that scale depth dynamically across `40L` (`R=1`), `64L` (`R=2`), and `88L` (`R=3`) by unlocking diverse frozen experts across passes; (2) **Step-0 IEEE-754 bit-identical NVIDIA `GDN-2-1M` (`arXiv:2605.22791`) + Bounded QED (`arXiv:2608.13668`) + Soft Negative Eigenvalues** mapped 1-to-1 onto `fla 0.6.0` `DPLR` kernels (`0` extra recurrent state bytes); and (3) pure `4.0×` YaRN `FP8 1.0× SharedKV` with untied per-pass `D-LSA` Ward-bucketed sparse attention. We have already completed `156.61` single-GPU hours of end-to-end engineering, negative-ablation forensics, Blackwell `sm_120` Triton kernel optimization, and live `1M`-token training on a single rented GPU; we now seek a focused multi-GPU allocation to execute controlled `s2_v2` + verifiable `s4_v2_rl` (SI-RSI) training and a rigorous FLOP-matched `d ∈ {1, 2, 3}` evaluation across 16 frontier 2026 benchmarks.

## 2. Systems Foundation & Single-GPU Evidence (Why This Work Is Already De-Risked)

### 2.1 Foundational Enabler: APOLLO Optimizer (`arXiv:2412.05270`) + `FusedStepOptimizer` on One `96 GB` Card

Training a `34.661B` looped MoE up to `131K–1M` tokens on a single **`96 GB GDDR7 ECC`** card without ZeRO/FSDP is enabled by **APOLLO (`arXiv:2412.05270`)** integrated into **`FusedStepOptimizer`** (`configs/base.yaml:89-97`, `src/qloop/train/optim.py:382-421`):
- **Exact Routing Rule:** `apollo: {enabled: true, rank: 256, scale_type: channel, scale: 1.0, update_proj_gap: 200, min_dim: 128}` via `apollo_torch.APOLLOAdamW` (with built-in faithful `ApolloAdamWFallback`) routes **every large 2D trainable matrix (`min(shape) >= 128`, not in `adamw_patterns`)** to low-rank `rank=256` SGD-like memory APOLLO, while 1D norms & narrow `<128`-dim adapters use `AdamW`/`AdamWLite` (with fp32 master copies for `bf16` weights).
- **Stage-by-Stage Honest Runtime Record:** `s1_loop_a6b` (`137` adapter/norm tensors, `18.41M` params) ran `fused=True` in-backward freeing `.grad` immediately (`adamw:AdamWLite x137`, `runs/s1_loop_a6b_run.log:55-58`). Starting at commit `3a17c0d`, `s1b_hssd_distill` / `s3*` / `s2*` / `s4*` switched to `fused=False` for an atomic whole-step non-finite guard + true global `clip_grad_norm_` while keeping `apollo.enabled: true`. In **`s2_joint_bptt`** (`PERF_PROFILE_S2.md:59`), `apollo_torch` actively ran **APOLLO (`rank=256`, channel-wise) on all `30` large 2D full-attention matrices (`k_proj`, `v_proj`, `o_proj` across `10L` = `104.86M` params; `0.186 GiB` vs `0.781 GiB` AdamW moments → `4.2×` savings, `8×` on `o_proj`)** alongside `767` fp32 `AdamW` + `100` bf16 `AdamWLite` tensors — and in `s4_v2_rl`, APOLLO covers both `10` `o_proj` + `120` `shared_expert` 2D matrices (`209.72M` params, saving `~1.02 GiB`).
- **Blackwell Vectorization:** `_foreach_norm(..., dtype=fp32)` collapses `897 → ~4` norm launches with `0.0` error (avoiding PyTorch's `0.24` bf16 error), reuses `_step_gnorm_sq` via `_foreach_mul_` for single-pass global clipping, batches `100` `AdamWLite` updates, and pins `non_blocking` APOLLO projection transfers.

## 3. HSSD v3.2 Post-Mortem & Lessons From Honest Negative Ablations

A central differentiator of our engineering methodology is catching silent failure modes early and converting negative ablations into principled architectural upgrades:

1. **HSSD v3.2 Post-Mortem → Why NVIDIA `GDN-2-1M` (`arXiv:2605.22791`) + Bounded QED (`arXiv:2608.13668`) Is the Obvious, Principled Move:** Our v1 pipeline replaced stock GDN with custom HSSD v3.2 (`[513,576]` SWA + `M=8` log-spaced `Top-2` STE surprise-gated pages + low-rank complex phase). Across `s1b → s3 → s2@500`, logs exposed every failure mode: (i) an $O(T)$ cumulative-phase Jacobian blowup (`6.12e+19`, `194,510` skips) fixed numerically via `chunk_size/T` scaling (`a0877a3`/`3a17c0d` → `0 / 1,100` skips); (ii) an irreversible **`+0.79` nat non-identity representation shock** (`0.919 → 1.705`); (iii) collapsed long-horizon vault pages (`vault_duty = 2e-4 → 3.26e-5` despite `distill_kl = 0.72`); (iv) failed `18K` needle retrieval **even under `LSA=dense`** (`truth_check_summary.json`, while `s1_loop_a6b` passed `16K` needle at `d=1/3` and `top1 = 1.0000` ruled out decode bugs); and (v) **`+5.17 GiB` 1M state overhead & `3.41 tok/s` `d=3` decode (`5.6×` slower than stock `19.22 tok/s`)**. Scientific lessons: enforce mandatory `0.0` Step-0 bit-identity, never rely on NLL without generative/needle truth-checks, audit datasets before mixing, and generalize mature pretrained DeltaNet manifolds onto production `fla 0.6.0` `DPLR` kernels — yielding **`GDN-2-1M + Bounded QED`** (`0.0` Step-0 diff, `0` extra state bytes, `1.02×` native decode, `47.1%` of HSSD params).
2. **Quarantining Upstream Tool-Call Poisoning (`fable51`) & Eliminating Eval Contamination:** Forensics caught `s1b` `d=3` emitting literal `{"query": "<search_query>"}` loops, traced to `100%` placeholder-redacted `tool_calls` in raw upstream `fable51` (`<bash_command>` `6,098×`, `<file_path>` `5,972×`, `<search_query>` `2,441×`). Auditing all sources removed `2,107` duplicates, `2` non-permissive repos, `247` poisoned rows, and `31` live 2026 benchmark leaks, while decoupled `working_notebook` synthesis lifted reasoning-trace quality from `4.1%` to `100.0%` (`197/197`).
3. **Transparent Unmeasured Scope:** Because `s3_v2` is currently at `step 450/600` (`512K–1M` peak recall `0.6514`, approaching our `0.70` auto-switch target) and `s2_v2` / `s4_v2_rl` have only passed live smoke/kernel verification, **we make zero claims on downstream 2026 frontier benchmarks yet**. Measuring those gains — and running the decisive ablations — is the exact purpose of the allocation below.
