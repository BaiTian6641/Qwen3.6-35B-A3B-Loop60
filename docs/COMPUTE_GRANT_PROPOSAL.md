## 1. Research Pitch & Motivation

Scaling test-time reasoning via long chains of thought alone hits severe KV-cache and serial-token bottlenecks, while training looped or ultra-long-context transformers from scratch requires industrial-scale pretraining budgets. We investigate a sharp, under-explored alternative: **can a frozen `35B` (`A3B`) NVFP4 hybrid MoE backbone (`unsloth/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`, `32.212B` routed experts 100% frozen and SHA-256 verified) be retrofitted with `< 0.6%` trainable parameters into a runtime-scalable `88`-virtual-layer looped reasoner supporting `1,048,576`-token (`1M`) context inside `10.65 GiB` of context state?** Our clean v2 architecture combines three orthogonal mechanisms: (1) a calibrated `[12,36)` ETD sandwich loop with mHC `LoopBridge` and per-pass router adapters that scale depth dynamically across `40L` (`R=1`), `64L` (`R=2`), and `88L` (`R=3`) by unlocking diverse frozen experts across passes; (2) **Step-0 IEEE-754 bit-identical `GDN-2-1M + Bounded QED + Soft Negative Eigenvalues`** (`arXiv:2608.13668`) mapped 1-to-1 onto `fla 0.6.0` `DPLR` kernels (`0` extra recurrent state bytes); and (3) pure `4.0×` YaRN `FP8 1.0× SharedKV` with untied per-pass `D-LSA` Ward-bucketed sparse attention. We have already completed `156.01` single-GPU hours of end-to-end engineering, negative-ablation forensics, Blackwell Triton kernel optimization, and live `1M`-token training on a single rented GPU; we now seek a focused multi-GPU allocation to execute controlled `s2_v2` + verifiable `s4_v2_rl` (SI-RSI) training and a rigorous FLOP-matched `d ∈ {1, 2, 3}` evaluation across 16 frontier 2026 benchmarks.

## 3. Lessons From Honest Negative Ablations

A central differentiator of our engineering methodology is catching silent failure modes early rather than masking them behind loss curves:

1. **Why We Replaced HSSD v3.2 With Step-0 Bit-Identical `GDN-2-1M + Bounded QED`:** Our v1 pipeline replaced stock GDN with HSSD v3.2 (`s1b → s3 → s2@500`). Although `s2_joint_bptt` lowered `131K` held-out NLL (`1.3508 → 1.1872`), an adversarial **Phase-A truth-check & 4-stage causal component swap** (`docs/experiment_logs/truth_check_summary.json`) before launching RL proved: (a) incremental decode parity was exact (`top1 = 1.0000`, uncached oracle reproduced degradation), (b) `88L` looping and `D-LSA` sparse attention were innocent (`s1_loop_a6b` passed `16K` needle at `d=1/3`; turning D-LSA dense yielded identical collapse), and (c) **HSSD v3.2 itself caused the failure** — leaving a permanent `+0.79` nat non-identity shock (`0.919 → 1.705`), dead long-horizon vault pages (`vault_duty = 3.26e-5`), `5.17 GiB` extra 1M state, and `3.41 tok/s` `d=3` decode (`5.6×` slower than stock `19.22 tok/s`). Dropping HSSD immediately delivered the `-0.630` nat (`46.7%` PPL) clean v2 win in `s3_v2`, and led to `GDN-2-1M + QED` via `fla` `DPLR`: exact `0.0` Step-0 identity, `0` extra state bytes, and `1.02×` native decode latency.
2. **Quarantining Upstream Tool-Call Poisoning (`fable51`) & Eliminating Eval Contamination:** Forensics caught `s1b` `d=3` emitting literal `{"query": "<search_query>"}` loops, traced to `100%` placeholder-redacted `tool_calls` in raw upstream `fable51` (`<bash_command>` `6,098×`, `<file_path>` `5,972×`, `<search_query>` `2,441×`). Auditing all sources removed `2,107` duplicates, `2` non-permissive repos, `247` poisoned rows, and `31` live 2026 benchmark leaks, while decoupled `working_notebook` synthesis lifted reasoning-trace quality from `4.1%` to `100.0%` (`197/197`).
3. **Transparent Unmeasured Scope:** Because `s3_v2` is currently at `step 425/600` (`512K–1M` peak recall `0.606`, latest `0.597`, still below our `0.70` auto-switch target) and `s2_v2` / `s4_v2_rl` have only passed live smoke/kernel verification, **we make zero claims on downstream 2026 frontier benchmarks yet**. Measuring those gains — and running the decisive ablations — is the exact purpose of the allocation below.
